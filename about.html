Author Journey

Dark fantasy, myth and the world of Avis

R.A. Crawford is a UK dark fantasy author and the creator of Project Avis. His fiction explores choice, inheritance, political tension, hidden history and the consequences of ancient power returning to a world that tried to bury it.

The War of Feather & Shadow opens The Raven’s Heir Trilogy and follows Kael from Winterhold into Aethelmar Academy, where the Dominion’s rival Houses, forbidden records and the erased legacy of House Raven begin to collide.

The wider Project Avis setting extends beyond the novels into the Aerie Dominion, maps, House lore, Blackfeather, the Trial of the Houses and the Royal Archive. These connected pieces are designed to let readers explore the world from different angles while keeping the books at the centre of the story.
