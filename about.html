Author Journey

Dark fantasy, myth and the world of Avis

R.A. Crawford is the author of The War of Feather & Shadow, Book One of The Raven’s Heir Trilogy. His fiction centres on choice, inheritance, political tension, hidden history and the consequences of ancient power returning to a world that tried to bury it.

The trilogy is set in the Aerie Dominion, where rival Houses, Aethelmar Academy and the erased legacy of House Raven shape Kael’s story. The wider Project Avis world also includes maps, lore, Blackfeather and the Royal Archive.
