The War of Feather & Shadow

Book One of The Raven’s Heir Trilogy by R.A. Crawford. A dark fantasy story of rival Houses, an elite academy, forbidden bloodlines, political intrigue and a history that powerful people worked to erase.

Kael has survived as a thief in the Gutter District with little reason to expect more from life. When he is drawn into Aethelmar Academy, he enters a world where five Great Houses train warriors, scholars and leaders—and where the forgotten name of House Raven is dangerous enough to be buried.

What kind of fantasy is it?

A dark fantasy novel built around Houses, secrets and an academy

The War of Feather & Shadow combines epic fantasy worldbuilding with an academy setting. The story moves through Aethelmar Academy and the wider Aerie Dominion, where House identity affects training, loyalty, politics and inheritance.

The central mystery concerns House Raven: a sixth House that official history says should not exist. Kael’s arrival at the academy brings him closer to that erased history, while Blackwing, the Warden and competing House interests pull the story into a wider conflict.

For readers looking for

Dark academy fantasy with a larger epic world

This is written for readers who enjoy fantasy academies but want the school to be part of a much larger political and mythic setting. Aethelmar is not isolated from the world: the rival Houses, their courts, bloodlines and old conflicts shape what happens inside it.

Readers will encounter House rivalries, secret histories, strange visions, ancient power, friendship across faction lines and a protagonist whose identity is tied to a truth the Dominion has tried to suppress.

The setting

Aethelmar Academy and the Aerie Dominion

The novel begins in Winterhold before opening into the wider Dominion. Aethelmar Academy trains gifted students from the Great Houses: Crow, Falcon, Hawk, Swan and Owl. House Raven survives only as forbidden history—until the past begins pushing back into the present.

About the author

R.A. Crawford — UK dark fantasy author

R.A. Crawford writes fantasy shaped by history, myth, inheritance, political tension and the choices people make when power becomes available to them. The War of Feather & Shadow is the first novel in The Raven’s Heir Trilogy.
