The Raven’s Heir Trilogy · Book One

The War of Feather & Shadow

The War of Feather & Shadow is a dark fantasy novel by UK author R.A. Crawford, set in a fractured Dominion ruled by rival Great Houses, ancient loyalties and a history that has been deliberately rewritten.

Five Great Houses stand at the centre of the Dominion: Crow, Falcon, Hawk, Swan and Owl. But there was once a sixth. House Raven has been erased from official history, reduced to forbidden myth. When Kael, a young thief carrying a mysterious spiral mark, is drawn into Aethelmar Academy, the truth begins to surface.

A dark fantasy novel of Houses, bloodlines and forbidden history

At Aethelmar Academy, students from the Dominion’s Houses are trained to become warriors, scholars, diplomats and leaders. Kael arrives from a very different world: the streets, the Gutter District and a life built on survival. His acceptance into the academy should offer escape. Instead, it places him at the centre of secrets powerful people have spent generations burying.

As strange visions, hidden records and old symbols begin to connect, Kael is forced to question what the Dominion teaches about its own past. The mystery of House Raven, the Blackwing order, rival House loyalties and the power behind the ancient thrones draw him deeper into a conflict that reaches far beyond the academy walls.

For readers who enjoy dark academy fantasy and epic worldbuilding

The novel combines an academy setting with political Houses, secret histories, magic, rebellion, friendship, betrayal and inherited power. Readers can explore the wider Project Avis world through the Aerie Dominion map, House lore, Blackfeather, the Trial of the Houses and other archive material on the official R.A. Crawford website.

If you are looking for a dark fantasy book with a magical academy, rival factions, hidden bloodlines and a long-form epic fantasy world, The War of Feather & Shadow is the opening volume of a planned trilogy that continues with The Avis Crown.

About R.A. Crawford

R.A. Crawford is a UK dark fantasy author and creator of Project Avis. His fiction draws on history, myth, inheritance, political rivalry and the consequences of power. The War of Feather & Shadow is the first book in The Raven’s Heir Trilogy.

Frequently asked questions

What is The War of Feather & Shadow about?

It follows Kael, a young thief drawn into Aethelmar Academy, where rival Great Houses, forbidden history and the erased legacy of House Raven begin to expose the truth about his own bloodline.

Who wrote The War of Feather & Shadow?

The novel is written by UK dark fantasy author R.A. Crawford.

Is The War of Feather & Shadow part of a series?

Yes. It is Book One of The Raven’s Heir Trilogy. Book Two is titled The Avis Crown.

Can I read a sample before buying?

Yes. The complete first chapter, “House Crow,” is available free on the official website.
