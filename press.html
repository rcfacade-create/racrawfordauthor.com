Full Biography

Author Biography

R.A. Crawford is the author of The Raven’s Heir Trilogy, an epic dark-fantasy series built around legacy, divided Houses, ancient power and the consequences of inheritance. His fiction draws on history and myth while placing character choices at the centre of political and personal conflict.

The trilogy opens with The War of Feather & Shadow, introducing Aethelmar Academy and a world where five noble Houses train their elite while older secrets begin to surface. Beyond the novels, Crawford continues to expand the Aerie Dominion through maps, archive material, lore, artwork and the Blackfeather card world.
