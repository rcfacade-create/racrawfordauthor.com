Book One · Available Now

The War of Feather & Shadow

A crown divides. A bloodline rises. A war begins.

At Aethelmar Academy, five noble Houses train their elite. But when ancient secrets surface and the Raven bloodline awakens, loyalty is tested and destinies collide.

Kael is drawn into a struggle of inheritance, power and fractured allegiance where every House carries its own secrets—and the return of Raven threatens to change the Dominion forever.
