The Trial of the Houses

Competition Terms & Conditions

Take the Free House Trial

Promoter: Crawford & Sons Publishers Limited

Instagram: @racrawfordauthor

Entries close: Saturday 28 November 2026, 11:59pm GMT

Winner announcement: Sunday 29 November 2026

Eligibility: UK residents aged 18+. Free entry.

1. Competition period

The giveaway opens when the official announcement appears on @racrawfordauthor and closes at 11:59pm GMT on Saturday 28 November 2026. Entries received afterwards will not qualify. The winner is scheduled to be announced on Sunday 29 November 2026.

2. Eligibility

Open to UK residents aged 18 or over. Employees and directors of Crawford & Sons Publishers Limited and their immediate families are excluded. No purchase or payment is necessary. One entry per person.

3. How to enter

  1. Visit the free Trial of the Houses and complete it.
  2. Save or download your personalised House Certificate.
  3. Share a picture of the certificate to your Instagram Story, mentioning @racrawfordauthor. If your Instagram account is private, send your certificate by direct message to @racrawfordauthor instead.
  4. Comment your assigned House (Crow, Owl, Hawk, Falcon, Swan or Raven) on the official competition post.

All steps must be completed by the closing time. Story entries must be visible to the promoter and mention the correct account. Since Stories expire, the promoter will keep a record of eligible entries as they are received. Private-account direct-message entries will be treated equally.

4. Prize

One winner will receive one signed paperback of The War of Feather & Shadow by R.A. Crawford, one Project Avis sticker collection, one collector's bookmark and one physical copy of Aethelmar in Colour. Standard UK postage is included. The prize is non-transferable and has no cash alternative; any necessary substitution will be of comparable or greater value.

5. Winner selection and contact

One winner will be selected at random from eligible, verified entries, using a demonstrably random computer-based method. The promoter will retain the eligible-entry list and draw record. The winner will be announced on Instagram on 29 November 2026 and contacted by direct message from @racrawfordauthor. The winner should respond within seven calendar days. If they do not respond or are ineligible, another eligible entry may be randomly drawn.

6. Delivery

The winner will be asked privately for a UK postal address. The promoter will cover standard UK delivery and aim to dispatch within 14 calendar days of receiving the delivery details, and award the prize within 30 days of closing.

7. Winner information

To demonstrate that a valid award took place, the promoter will make available appropriate winner information in accordance with the UK CAP Code. The winner can object to the publication of their information, or request that its publication be reduced, by contacting the promoter. Relevant information may still need to be supplied to the Advertising Standards Authority if challenged. Full names, photographs and certificates will not be reused for other promotion without permission.

8. Personal data

Personal data submitted for the competition, including Instagram handles, certificate images and the winner's delivery details, will be used to check eligibility, administer entries, select and contact the winner, deliver the prize and meet legal obligations. It will not be used for unrelated marketing without an appropriate lawful basis. Records will be kept only as long as reasonably required for these purposes and complaints. See the website’s policies or contact hello@racrawfordauthor.com with privacy questions.

9. Fair participation

Fraudulent, automated, duplicate or misleading entries may be disqualified on reasonable evidence. Entrants must use a genuine certificate generated through the official website. Entries may be checked against comments, Story mentions and direct messages. Any necessary changes will be communicated fairly and will not disadvantage entrants without good reason.

10. Instagram disclaimer

This promotion is organised by Crawford & Sons Publishers Limited and is not sponsored, endorsed, administered by or associated with Instagram or Meta. By entering, participants release Instagram from responsibility for the promotion to the extent allowed by law. Instagram’s own terms continue to apply.

11. General and contact

The promotion is governed by the laws of England and Wales, subject to any mandatory consumer protections. Nothing in these terms limits statutory rights. For questions, contact hello@racrawfordauthor.com or @racrawfordauthor.
