A fantasy world divided by Houses

The Aerie Dominion is the setting of R.A. Crawford’s Raven’s Heir Trilogy. Its cities, courts and academies are shaped by the Great Houses: Crow, Falcon, Hawk, Swan and Owl. Official history recognises five. Older secrets point towards a sixth—House Raven.

Book One begins in Winterhold and leads towards Aethelmar Academy, where gifted students train as warriors, scholars, diplomats and future leaders. The academy sits at the heart of House politics, inherited duty and the mystery surrounding the erased Raven bloodline.
